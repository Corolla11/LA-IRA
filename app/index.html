      Parameters (assumptions)

      Duration, reserve and stop time update instantly. Routing parameters or algorithm changes recompute the route. D* Lite currently starts a fresh search for each leg.

      Sources and limits

      • Image: HiRISE curiosity_hirise_mosaic, zoom 16, NASA Mars Trek.
      • Elevation and slope: HiRISE DTM Gale_DEM_SMG_1m, in ~2.4 m cells. Rocks and steps smaller than ~3 m do not appear.
      • Minerals: CRISM MTRDR FRT0000B6F1 (2008), HYD, PHY and CAR browse products. Alignment with HiRISE is approximate (offsets of tens of meters are possible).
      • Radiation: Curiosity RAD, E dose of 6.55 µGy/h measured on sols 4669–4673 at the rover location. The map is an estimate: that value multiplied by the visible sky fraction (horizon computed from the DTM, ~390 m radius), relative to that at the measured point.
      • Thermal: THEMIS night IR (~100 m). Sand = coldest 30% of the area, as a regional indication.
      • Traverse: MSL_waypoints.json, sols –.
      • Speed (adapted Tobler function), slope limits, reserve and stop times are adjustable assumptions.
